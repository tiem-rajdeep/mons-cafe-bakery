Our Story

Made with Love,
Baked to Perfection

Welcome to Mon's Café & Bakery — a home bakery born from a mother's love and a passion for creating sweet moments. Every cake we craft is made with the finest ingredients, real care, and the kind of attention you only find in a home kitchen.

From dreamy bento cakes and layered tub cakes to festive Christmas cakes & grand 2–3 tier celebration cakes, we specialize in turning your vision into reality. We also bake fresh brownies, artisan pizzas, buns, and serve ice cream tubs — all made with love.
